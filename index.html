Differential Builder

This is a study aid, not a diagnosis. It matches the words you type against what this notebook already says about each condition — the case presentations, the reference notes, the exam findings. It has no access to your patient, no probabilities, and no knowledge of anything outside this site. Read the five it offers as prompts to think with, and take the workup it suggests to your own references before you order anything.
Or pick symptoms from the list
How this works, and what it cannot do

It is term matching, not reasoning. Every condition in this notebook carries a weight for each of the 118 symptom terms in the vocabulary, derived from its own name and aliases, the case presentations filed under it, its therapeutics note, its reference sections, and the physical-exam findings that name it — plus a curated seed table of classic presentations written for this tool. Your text is scanned for those terms; the conditions are scored, normalised so a verbose entry cannot outrank a precise one, and the top five are shown with the terms each one matched on.

What it cannot do. It has no prior probabilities, so a rare condition that matches four terms will outrank a common one that matches three. It does not know age, sex, duration, severity, examination findings, past history or medication. It cannot exclude anything. It only knows the 186 conditions and topics in this notebook — a condition that is not in the Conditions tab cannot appear here, and that absence is not evidence.

Red flags are pattern rules, not a triage system. Fifteen combinations raise a banner — chest pain with breathlessness, melaena, thoughts of self-harm, headache with fever. The absence of a banner means nothing; a patient can be critically unwell and trip none of them.

The workup under each condition is not new content. It is the same labs, imaging, pharmaceuticals, supplements, botanicals, therapeutics, lifestyle changes and herbs already attached to that condition in the other tabs, gathered in one place. Their provenance and their cautions are the ones stated there.

Homeopathic Remedy Differentiator

Running tally

How the tool decides, and where the material comes from

A study tool, not a treatment plan — and not evidence-based medicine. Homeopathic remedies at potency contain no measurable active substance, and systematic reviews have not found them to work beyond placebo. Nothing here is a diagnosis or a substitute for medical care. Anyone with a high fever, breathing difficulty, severe or worsening pain, a head injury, a wound that will not stop bleeding, a stiff neck with a rash, dehydration in a child, or any symptom that frightens them should be seen by a clinician now. Use this as a way to learn and compare classical remedy pictures, and verify every result against a proper materia medica and repertory.

The method. It follows the approach of C.M. Boger's A Synoptic Key of the Materia Medica (1915, public domain): rank the generals — manner of onset, thermal reaction, thirst, time of aggravation, what motion, pressure, open air and position do, and the state of the mind — above the local symptoms, and let the differences between remedies, rather than a count of matching symptoms, decide the case.

The scoring. Every answer carries a grade for each remedy it points to: 3 a keynote, characteristic of that remedy; 2 strong; 1 present; − a counter-indication. Scores are summed, so a single keynote can outweigh several weak agreements — which is the point of the method.

Choosing the next question. After each answer the tool scores every question still unasked by how far apart it would drive the remedies currently in contention, weighted by how likely each of those is, and asks the one that separates them most. Skipped questions count for nothing rather than against.

The generals are asked of every case. Fourteen constitutional axes — onset, thermal state, the state of the mind, causation, the hour, what open air, motion and the weather do, thirst, sweat, prostration, company, food and sleep — are woven into every complaint alongside its own local questions, and the interview will not stop early until at least three of them have been answered, however clear the local symptoms look. That is the method's central claim: a case is decided on the generals, and a remedy that fits the complaint but contradicts them is the wrong remedy.

Remedies outside the pool. Each complaint carries a pool of remedies it usually calls for, but every answer is scored against all 148. When the generals push a remedy from outside that pool level with the leader — on the general questions alone, with the local symptoms counting for nothing — it is shown separately as a prompt to look again.

The material. The remedy pictures, the modality axes and the differentiations were written for this tool from classical materia medica held in common across the standard nineteenth- and twentieth-century texts. No text is reproduced from Boger or from any other work, and this index carries none of the authority of the sourced dosing data in the other tabs.

What it cannot do. It cannot examine anyone, cannot recognise a red flag, has no view on potency or repetition, and knows only the remedies and conditions loaded into it. A remedy it does not carry can easily be the right one.
