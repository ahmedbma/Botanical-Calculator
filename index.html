Assumptions & formulas
  • 1 tsp = 5 ml · 0.5 tsp = 2.5 ml · 1 oz = 30 ml · 1 ml = 25 gtt (varies with the dropper)

  • ml to dispense = dispensed total × share of formula.

Maximum Doses — Low-Dose Botanicals

Upper limits for low-dose (potentially toxic) herbs. Doses vary with the patient, the dilution and the potency of the batch; treat every figure as approximate.

Liquid herbal extractDilutionSingle (ml)Single (gtt) Chronic daily (ml)Acute daily (ml)Long-term use
  • Maximum acute daily dose assumes every-2-hours dosing over a 16-hour waking day; it generally should not be kept up more than a few days.
  • Maximum chronic daily dose is based on three times daily.
  • Doses vary by patient — general health status and body size among other variables.
  • Different dilutions give different doses, and natural extracts vary in potency batch to batch.
  • All doses are approximate, not absolute. Drops assume 25 gtt per ml.
